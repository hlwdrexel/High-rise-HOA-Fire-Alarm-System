# Detailed Bill of Materials - High-Rise Fire Alarm System

## Project basis
- Building: 10 stories, 25 condo units per floor, 250 units total
- Unit layout: 2BR / 2Bath condo units, commonly 1-2 smoke detectors per unit plus common-area devices
- Design basis used for estimate: residential unit detection, corridor/hallway notification, stairwell and lobby coverage, fire alarm control panel, and emergency communications support
- Estimate basis: 2026 USD, distributor pricing, typical not-to-exceed pricing for a mid-sized high-rise residential installation; excludes permit fees, contractor overhead, labor, conduit, box fill, and commissioning

## Budget summary
- Base equipment estimate: $128,000 to $188,000 USD
- Typical installed total (equipment + labor + commissioning): $220,000 to $340,000 USD

## Recommended procurement note
This is a typical 2026 budgetary estimate for a 250-unit high-rise residential fire alarm system using Honeywell NOTIFIER and System Sensor components. Pricing is based on distributor and project pricing trends and may vary significantly by region, quantity, product revisions, and local code requirements.

For final approvals, request an updated quote from an authorized Honeywell/Notifier distributor or fire alarm integrator for the exact devices, cabinet config, and code-required notification levels.
